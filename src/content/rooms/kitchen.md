## What you're looking at

This is the house's **kitchen** — the room where the family's food was prepared and where, in many such houses, the family also ate.

## Why it matters

In the nineteenth century the kitchen was the busiest room in any farmhouse. Three meals a day, every day, for a farm family and often for farmhands too — water hauled, wood split, pots tended, bread baked. The kitchen is where the house's daily rhythm actually lived.

## What to notice

- The **placement** of the kitchen — sometimes a separate outbuilding to keep heat and fire away from the house, sometimes a room within it.
