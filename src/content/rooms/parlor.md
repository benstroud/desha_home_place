## What you're looking at

This is the house's **parlor** — the more formal of the two pens, where a frontier family kept its best things and received its guests.

## Why it matters

In a double-pen dogtrot, one room was the working, everyday space and the other the "company" room. The parlor held the family's finest furniture, its portrait photographs, and its most important occasions — weddings, funerals, visits from the preacher, and the rare formal call.

## What to notice

- Look for the signs of a formal room: the better finish, the mantel, the placement near the front door.
- ▲ *Family note:* this is the room to fill with the family's own story — heirlooms, portraits, and the occasions this room has seen.
