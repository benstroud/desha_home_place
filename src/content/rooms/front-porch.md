## What you're looking at

The main facade of the house carries a **side gable roof** with a **projecting gabled roof at its center** — a covered entry that welcomes you into the dogtrot between the two pens.

## Why it matters

This is where the house says hello. On a farm in the White River bottoms, the front porch was where neighbors were greeted, where the day's work was discussed, and where the family watched the road.

## What to notice

- The **center gable** marks the front door even from a distance — a deliberate, graceful touch on a working farmhouse.
