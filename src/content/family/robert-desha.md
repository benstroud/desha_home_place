R.M. Desha — **Captain Robert Montgomery Desha** (1796–1822) — was Franklin's father, and the family
member the National Register record for this house associates with settling at **Helena,
Arkansas**, on the Mississippi River. He served in the Marine Corps and was later appointed
Quartermaster of the Marines' Mississippi region.

He was a son of **Joseph Desha**, the ninth governor of Kentucky, and thus a nephew of
**Benjamin Desha**, for whom Desha County is named. He died of yellow fever in **New Orleans** in
1822, when Franklin was a small boy. Franklin's mother, **Frances Ann Ferebee** (1797–1839), later
married **Hartwell Boswell** of Batesville. Boswell's first wife, **Rachel Harriet Desha**, was
Robert's sister.

A different Robert Desha — the Tennessee congressman and **brother** of Governor Joseph Desha — was
Robert Montgomery Desha's **uncle**, and so Franklin's great-uncle. He was a captain in the War of
1812 and represented Tennessee's 5th district in the U.S. House of Representatives (1827–1831)
before moving to Mobile, Alabama, where he died in 1849. He and Franklin's father were two separate
men ([Robert Desha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Desha), Wikipedia).
