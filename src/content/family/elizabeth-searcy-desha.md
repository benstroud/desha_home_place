Elizabeth Searcy married Franklin Desha, and together they made this house a home.

She was the daughter of **Richard Searcy**, the first clerk and judge in the Arkansas Territory —
the man for whom both the city of **Searcy** and **Searcy County** are named. Through her, the
house joins the Desha and Searcy families, two of the most important names in early Arkansas.

The family's stewardship of the house — from Franklin and Elizabeth through the Stroud and
Vandiver lines to the present day — is the thread that ties 1847 to now.
