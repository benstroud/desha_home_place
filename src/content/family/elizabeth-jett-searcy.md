Elizabeth Jett Searcy was the wife of **Franklin Desha** and the daughter of **Richard Searcy**,
the first clerk and judge of the Arkansas Territory, for whom the city of **Searcy** and
**Searcy County** are named.

She is shown here as a young woman. Together she and Franklin raised their family in the house
he built in **1847**, which passed down through their descendants — the Stroud and Vandiver
lines — and remains a family home today.
