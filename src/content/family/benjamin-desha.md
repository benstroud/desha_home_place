Benjamin Desha was Franklin's uncle and the namesake of **Desha County, Arkansas**.

He was a veteran of the **War of 1812**, wounded at the **Battle of Mackinac Island**. In 1822
President James Monroe appointed him receiver of public moneys for the **Arkansas Territory**,
bringing the Desha family west from Kentucky. He died in 1835, and three years later the
Arkansas legislature created **Desha County** (December 12, 1838) in his honor.

His brother's grandson — Franklin Desha — built the house this site celebrates.
